If you don't know how to play, here are the basics!

GAMEPLAY

In this game, there are waves. As the waves get higher, the enemy gets faster and stronger. You beat a wave by getting to length 11. But as you get longer, it is easier for the enemy to cut you.

AND ONCE THE ENEMY CUTS YOU, EVERYTHING FROM THE PART IT CUT, TO THE TIP OF YOUR TAIL!

UPGRADES

The snake game is full of stats that you can upgrade. These upgrades consist of speed, head strength, tail strength, and knowledge. Speed is used to, you guessed it, increase the speed of your snake. Head strength helps with immunity against anything lower than your head strength. Tail strenght is the same, but it is harder to buy upgrades, and knowledge is used to skip waves and to slow down the timing of the game, to make it more controllable.
